PLACEDON — FONT FILES

DISPLAY / HEADLINE FONT
Originally specified: Freight Display, Canela, or GT Sectra — all three are paid,
commercially licensed typefaces. I have not included files for them; downloading
them from anywhere other than the foundry/a paid license would be piracy.

Included instead: Fraunces-Variable.ttf — a free, open-source (SIL OFL) serif with
a similar "ink weight" editorial character. It's a genuinely close substitute, not
a placeholder — usable as-is for anything shown externally.

If you already own a license for Freight Display, Canela, or GT Sectra, just drop
the .ttf/.otf files into this folder alongside Fraunces and use whichever you have
rights to.

CITATION / VERBATIM TEXT FONT
Both originally specified options — IBM Plex Mono and Courier Prime — are free and
open source. Included: IBM Plex Mono (Regular, Medium, SemiBold).

LICENSES
Both included families are SIL Open Font License 1.1 — free for personal and
commercial use, including embedding in products and marketing materials. License
text included: Fraunces-OFL-LICENSE.txt, IBMPlexMono-OFL-LICENSE.txt.

BODY / SUPPORTING TEXT FONT
No specific family was ever locked in for this one — just "a clean sans-serif with
wide letter-spacing for small print." Not included here since it was never pinned
down. Inter or Archivo (both free, Google Fonts) would fit the existing palette if
you want one specified.
