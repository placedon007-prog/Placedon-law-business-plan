## Purposes, consent, and legal grounds

Your required form consent is limited to the chosen request. Waitlist consent covers recording interest and sending access updates; pilot consent covers assessing and replying to the enquiry. A separate unchecked option covers occasional development updates. Refusing that optional choice does not prevent either request.

Optional analytics requires a separate affirmative choice. Closing the banner, continuing to browse, or submitting a form does not supply analytics consent. You may reject optional analytics and still use the site.

For processing subject to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, `2023`, we will identify the applicable consent or permitted-use ground and the provisions then in force. The Indian framework must not be treated as containing the GDPR's general legitimate-interests ground. Its commencement arrangements and implementing rules require a current applicability review. [Official Act](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf) · [Official rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)

Where the GDPR applies, the proposed grounds are consent for updates and analytics; necessary steps requested before a possible individual contract where that ground fits the pilot enquiry; and documented legitimate interests for proportionate site security and business-contact correspondence where those interests are not overridden. A specific legal obligation may justify required records. The operator must document the actual ground for each purpose; a business enquiry does not automatically establish a contract with the individual sender. [GDPR text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32016R0679)

We will not use request data for an unrelated purpose without assessing the legal ground and providing any required notice or consent choice. This proposed website process does not make automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you.

| Consent evidence, privacy-request records, and unsubscribe suppression entries | Only the minimal record needed to demonstrate or honour a choice; proposed maximum 3 years after closure, subject to a documented lawful basis and earlier deletion when no longer needed |
| Backups containing deleted records                                             | Expire within 90 days after deletion from active systems, if backups are used; restored data must have deletions reapplied                                                                |

Sending an email, recording an open, or collecting a page view does not renew the contact-retention period. We will not keep an entire enquiry to preserve an unsubscribe choice. A restricted suppression record may retain only what is needed to avoid sending further updates.

If a legal duty, order, or specific dispute requires longer retention, we will isolate the relevant material, record the reason and review point, and delete it when that reason ends. It will not be retained for continued marketing. Deletion instructions must reach the processors that hold the data.
