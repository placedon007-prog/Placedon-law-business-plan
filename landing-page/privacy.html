Privacy policy

What we collect
and why.

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Who we are

Placedon is a statutory compliance intelligence tool for Indian private limited companies, currently in early development. We are collecting information from interested practitioners before the product is built. This policy describes what we collect, how we use it, and what we don't do with it.

What we collect

We collect only what you give us directly, through two forms:

  • Waitlist form: full name, email address, company or firm name (optional), role, and an optional compliance question.
  • Account registration: full name, email address, password (stored as a salted hash — we never see the plaintext), and role.

We do not use cookies for tracking. We do not use analytics scripts. We do not fingerprint your browser. The session cookie we set after login exists only to keep you signed in and contains no personal data.

Why we collect it

  • To contact you when early access becomes available.
  • To prioritise which provisions to verify first, based on the compliance questions you share.
  • To understand who our users are — practising CS, CA, founder, or other — so the product is built for the right person.

We will not send you marketing email unrelated to Placedon.

How it is stored

During this development phase, data is stored in files on a local server operated by the Placedon team. It is not hosted on a public cloud or shared with third parties. When we move to a hosted environment, this policy will be updated.

Passwords are hashed using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 with a random salt and 260,000 iterations. The plaintext password is never written to disk.

Your rights

You can ask us to delete your data at any time. Send a request to saiyamupadhyay200@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my data" and we will remove your record within 7 days and confirm by reply.

You can also ask to see what we hold about you. The same email address applies.

Disclaimer

Placedon is a research tool, not a law firm. Nothing on this site or in any output from Placedon constitutes legal advice. The tool finds and cites statute — the legal conclusion is yours to draw, with your counsel.
