Why we exist

Every Indian company is one missed section away from a penalty it didn't see coming.

Statutory compliance in India is either slow and expensive — a Company Secretary or Chartered Accountant retainer, hours per question — or fast and dangerous, because generic AI answers questions it was never verified against. Neither gives a founder or a compliance professional what they actually need: a direct answer, traced to the exact provision, with an honest admission when the answer isn't known.

Slow

Blind
Filing software tracks deadlines it's told about. It doesn't tell you what applies to you as you grow.
Confident
Generic AI sounds certain whether or not it's right — and a confident wrong answer is worse than no answer.
What we cover

Two statutes, chosen deliberately — not everything, on purpose.

Both are central, national statutes — one verification pass covers all of India, not a patchwork of state rules. Both carry real, immediate consequences for the companies that miss them.

Financial law

Companies Act, 2013

AGM timing, annual returns, financial statements, board meetings, small-company and CSR thresholds — the statutory calendar every private limited company runs on, computed from your own facts, not a generic template.

Digital law

DPDP Act, 2023

Data Fiduciary obligations, breach notification timing, and Significant Data Fiduciary status. Phase II compliance obligations become operational in November 2026 — this isn't a future problem.

How it works

We don't guess.

If we can't point to the exact section behind an answer, we say so instead of answering anyway. That refusal is the entire difference between a tool that sounds confident and one that's actually accountable — and it's the one thing about us that can't be faked.
