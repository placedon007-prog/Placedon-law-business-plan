Why it exists

The signing CS bears the risk.
The tools don't.

Statutory compliance in India is either slow and expensive — a retainer, hours per question, a two-day wait for a two-minute answer — or fast and dangerous, because generic AI sounds certain whether or not it has read the provision. Neither option was designed for the person who certifies the filing.

01
Slow

A CS retainer or lawyer's hourly rate turns a two-minute applicability question into a two-day wait — and the question still has to go back when the Act changes.

02
Blind

Filing software tracks deadlines it's been told about. It doesn't tell you which thresholds apply as your company grows, or which amendment changed them last.

03
Confident

Generic AI answers with certainty whether or not it has read the provision. A confident wrong answer has a specific cost: your name on the adjudication order.

How it works

Two outputs.
No third option.

A query enters. Either a citation exits — the section quoted verbatim, the arithmetic shown, the status declared — or a refusal exits. Placedon does not fill the gap with inference.
